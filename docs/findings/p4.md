## 1. Summary

- **Option A cannot test P4 within certification.** This is the plan's fourth outcome (Section 8: "class 2, still vacuous"). Under the plan, only B2 can settle P4 (#7).
  - 10 of the 33 candidate conditions were certified as irrelevant:
    - F6s κ noise at small scales (3 members);
    - all four K-str levels;
    - widening only (W+) at 0.5 and 1 m.
  - No wobble member was certified at any amplitude.
  - They reach d_OOD of only 0.74–1.53. S2's failing single-factor F1 conditions sit at 2.01 and 2.37, and S3's at 2.75.
  - So S2 and S3 stay class 2 with no F6 perturbation at or above their failing condition's d_OOD (`n_f6_at_or_above` 0). The recipe claim is again "class 2" from those two vacuous classifications.
- **Neither specialist was shown to be fragile either.** On a certified condition, S2's lowest C is 0.71 and S3's is 0.78. Class 1 needs C ≤ 0.5 at or below the failing d_OOD.
- **The one post hoc extension did not change this.** K-str at 16e-3 m⁻¹ was certified; 32e-3 and 64e-3 were not. At 16e-3, S2's C falls to 0.56, just above the 0.5 needed for class 1, while its d_OOD stays at 0.87. The classes are unchanged.
- **Why the reach is so short (descriptive).** P4's d_OOD window runs from the tier D start to 50 m past the corner start, about 250–450 m of approach.
  - Changes in that window large enough to move d_OOD fail certification. Wobbles and κ noise near the corner move the corner's optimum by more than 1%. Widening by 2 m or more makes the generalists crash.
  - K-str does pass at every level, but its footprint falls inside the d_OOD window at only 2 of the 9 corners.
- **Expected directions** (Section 5 has the details):
  - **Borne out:** E1, all three parts (E1a only because no wobble member was certified at all); E2's rise of d_OOD with amplitude (ρ = 1 in all 9 pairs of specialist and sub-ladder); E5, S1 class 6.
  - **Not borne out:** E2's reach (no certified condition reaches 2.37 for S2 or 2.75 for S3); E3, S2 class 1 (it is class 2, vacuous); E4, S3 class 1 or 6 (it is class 2, vacuous).
  - **Not applicable:** E6.

**What the table shows (descriptive):**
- **The s-OCP rule is the main filter.**
  - Even a 0.3 m in-phase wobble moves the corner's v\*_min by more than 1% at 4 of 9 corners, by up to 2.8%. It runs through the corner, so it changes the corridor there.
  - F6s κ noise also covers the corner region, so it loses corners as its peak grows: it is certified at 7–9 corners up to a peak of 1.5e-3 m⁻¹, and at 6 or fewer from 2e-3 on, in both sub-ladders. (Amendment 2's "from scale 3 on" is loose: knoise1e-3 already drops to 6 corners at scale 2.)
  - K-str leaves the corner region untouched. It moves v\*_min by more than 1% only at the slowest corner (corner 14, v\*_min 15.5 m/s; up to 1.9%), through its approach.
- **The generalists filter large changes.** Wobbles and widening make the generalists crash on Hockenheim's own corners:
  - every wobble of 0.9 m or more fails their rule (median C_G 0–0.4), and so does one of the two 0.6 m wobbles (0.75); every wobble of 1.2 m or more gives 0;
  - widening fails from 2 m, and W+ at 4 m gives a median C_G of 0.17.
  
  That a wider road makes the generalists fail is an aside worth noting for the width work. It is not examined here.
- **Step 1's registered F6 rows, for comparison** (descriptive, from step 1's stored s-OCP keys): they would pass the s-OCP rule at 7–8 of the 9 corners. Wobble 0.15 and 0.3 m pass at 7, κ noise at 8.

**The nearest certified condition** to every failing F1 condition is W+ at 1 m. For S2 it sits at 1.53 (C 0.71), against 2.01 and 2.37. For S3 it sits at 1.34 (C 1.0), against 2.75.

**Why K-str's d_OOD barely moves.** K-str needs straight runs longer than 200 m that lie at least 50 m from every corner. On Hockenheim's braking corners, those runs mostly lie after the scored corner.
- At corners 7 and 14 the noise lies inside the d_OOD window, which runs from the start to s_c0 + 50 m.
- At corners 4, 16 and 17 it lies only in the completion window, up to s_c1 + 250 m.
- At corners 0, 1, 3 and 8 it lies beyond both windows.

So K-str can lower C, and it does: at 16e-3, S2 fails corners 4, 7, 14 and 17. But it can move a median d_OOD taken over 7–9 corners only a little. This is a property of the footprint and of P4's d_OOD window, not of the specialists.

## 6. The post hoc ladder extension (Amendment 2)

Section 10 of the plan allows one ladder extension when no certified condition reaches a failing condition's d_OOD.

**What was extended.** K-str, because it was the only family certified at its top rung. It was continued at 16e-3, 32e-3 and 64e-3 m⁻¹, with its own build, certification, freeze and runs.

**Results:**
- **Checks.** All passed again.
- **Certification.** 16e-3 is certified at all 9 corners, with median C_G 1.0. 32e-3 fails the generalists' rule (0.875). 64e-3 keeps v\*_min within 1% at only 6 corners.
- **Specialists at 16e-3.**
  - S1: C 0.89 at d_OOD 0.91.
  - S2: C 0.56 at d_OOD 0.87. That is the closest any certified condition came to class 1: at or below S2's failing d_OOD, but 0.06 above the 0.5 that class 1 needs.
  - S3: C 0.67 at d_OOD 0.91.
- **Classes.** Unchanged: 6, 2 and 2. S2's and S3's class 2 stays vacuous.

The trend on K-str is consistent with fragility. On the same certified road changes, the specialists' completion falls (S3: 1.0, 1.0, 0.875, 0.78, 0.67 across the five K-str levels) while the generalists' median C_G stays at 1.0. But it never reaches P4's class 1 threshold. The extension was chosen after the planned results were seen, so it is post hoc and decides nothing.

## 7. What it means (plan Section 8)

- **For S2 and S3: "class 2, still vacuous".**
  - Option A cannot tell memorisation from fragility within certification. Every road change that is irrelevant in the plan's sense (the corner's optimum unchanged, the generalists complete it) stays at d_OOD ≤ 1.53.
  - The failing F1 conditions sit at 2.0–2.75. Their own road changes are large (a straightened road after the corner, a constant 12 m width).
  - The two readings stay unseparated, as after step 1. Each specialist's completion collapses under one single-factor change of Hockenheim's road, and certified irrelevant changes leave it mostly intact (C ≥ 0.71). The protocol's tier G rule still reads S2's and S3's null-straight crashes as evidence for class 1 on synthetic stimuli.
- **For S1: class 6, unchanged,** as Section 2 of the plan said it must be.
- **For the A-or-B2 decision (#7).** The plan's own table sends this outcome to B2: 5 fresh specialists under a new pre-registration, about 1 h on the A6000. Option A has been run as planned. Within the plan's certification, nothing further on the existing specialists can make P4's class 2 non-vacuous.
  - The binding constraints are the 1% s-OCP rule and P4's d_OOD window (the approach, about 250–450 m).
  - A B2 pre-registration would need F6 perturbations that both pass certification and move d_OOD in that window. That is a design question for #7, and it is not solved here.
- **For `protocol-v1` (#14).** Its wording for the specialists cannot come from option A's result. That result leaves "context-keyed (vacuously) or fragile" open, so it has to come from B2 or stay open.

## 8. Not covered, and limits

- **Corner-by-corner reporting.** The plan asks for it only for class 6. It is in `outputs/p4/specialists/*_runs.npz` and `c_vs_dood.csv`, and is not tabulated here.
- **The generalists' failures on wider roads** (W+ ≥ 2 m) and on wobbles were not examined further.
- **One bookkeeping fix after a result was seen.** In the extension run, the W+ contingency rule first ran on the absent W+ members and recorded "triggered". The rule was then marked not applicable there, and the stage was re-run from its cached rollouts. Nothing was solved or driven again, and no certification changed (the extension has no contingency members).
- **The 12 refused solves.** IPOPT refused 12 s-OCP solves outright, at 6 corners, for F6s wobble0.6 × 6 and × 8, because the bounds were ill-posed. They count as not converged, so as not certified. They are listed as errors in `socp.json`.

# X-2 and X-3: at G2's real crash corners the line follows a corner-wide edge shift strongly (0.75), but the real change's apex-local shape only weakly (0.07). Locality explains the low real-road ratio. A 1 m local intrusion crashes G2 where a 1 m corner-wide one never does (exploratory)

Results of 30 Oct 2026. Plan: [x2-plan.md](x2-plan.md), committed and pushed in `8d81ef2` before anything here was computed. Code: `scripts/x2x3_exploratory.py`, which imports step 2's frozen `src/apexzero/fields` and `scripts/locality_exploratory.py` read-only. Results: `outputs/x2x3/results.json` (committed). Background: [locality.md](locality.md) and [receptive-fields-step2.md](receptive-fields-step2.md) Section 7.7 (H3).

**Every number here is exploratory** and comes from `results.json`.
- **Network and roads:** G2 (step 2's pinned `dr-pilot-v2-tilt30`) at the 12 unit-phases of H3: Norisring 874, Melbourne 2531 and Catalunya 4168, phases 0–3. All three corners are R-dev right-handers.
- **Recipe:** H3's own starts, roads, width change and 512-step loop.
- **Cost:** about 20 s on the Mac.

## 1. Summary

- **X-2 reading (the plan's rule): "locality explains the low real-road ratio".**
  - At these same real corners and start states, G2's corner-wide gain is high: the trapezoid G has a median of **0.75** (T; per corner 0.68, 0.79, 0.76), against 0.83 on synthetic corners.
  - Short apex bumps are followed only weakly: L/e is 0.105 at σ_b = 2 m and 0.210 at 4 m (S, the median of both, is 0.156).
  - The real inside-width change is followed at a median **0.069**. That reproduces H3's 0.07 exactly, unit-phase by unit-phase.
- **The real changes are sharper than a 2 m bump** at 7 of the 12 unit-phases (Melbourne phases 0–2 and all four at Catalunya). At the other five they act like a bump of σ_b = 3.0–6.6 m (Norisring, and Melbourne phase 3). Norisring, where the line follows most (0.25), is where the real change is broadest.
- **The kernel is as on synthetic corners.** The response to an 8 m bump peaks 10 m before the closest point (0.49), against 0.40 at it and 0.17 10 m after. The line trails the edge by about 10 m.
- **X-3 reading (the plan's rule): first order "holds" for the corner-wide trapezoid and "fails" for the σ_b = 4 m bump and the X = 5 m ramp.** The failure is entirely on the widening side:
  - **For intrusions** (the edge coming in), the finite-size response stays within 0.004–0.033 of the linear one up to 0.5 m, for all three profiles.
  - **For local widening** of 0.5–1 m, the finite L/e exceeds the linear one by 0.06–0.55. The closest point then moves a median 2.4–4.9 m away from s\*, against at most 0.9 m otherwise (a diagnostic added after the first run, Section 4). So c_min stops reading the widened spot.
- **One metre of intrusion:**
  - **Local:** a 1 m intrusion at the apex crashes G2 at 10 of 12 unit-phases (the 4 m bump) or 8 of 12 (the 5 m ramp).
  - **Corner-wide:** a 1 m intrusion across the whole corner crashes it at none.
  - **Why:** the line gives way by about 0.75 m to a corner-wide intrusion, but by only about 0.2 m to a local one, which uses up the 0.64–0.81 m buffer.

## 5. What it means (the lead's reading, not tested)

- **The puzzle between step 2's corner-wide G (about 0.8) and H3's real-road 0.07 is resolved for G2's three crash corners: the states and corners are not the cause.**
  - At those corners and states, a corner-wide shift is followed at 0.75.
  - The real change is followed at 0.07 because it is concentrated within a few metres of the apex, sharper than a 2 m bump at 7 of 12 unit-phases.
  - The kernel shows why. The line answers the edge about 10 m late and over a window of tens of metres, so a change confined to the apex is over before the line reacts.
- **The crash mechanism follows directly.** A local intrusion of about 1 m crashes G2 where a corner-wide intrusion of the same size does not.
- **This is the target GW-5's bump arm trains for** (`gw5-plan.md`). The question there is whether training on local width bumps raises the short-scale response.
- **Limits:**
  - one network and three corners (12 unit-phases, four per corner);
  - G2's start states;
  - X-2 is first order, and X-3 shows that first order overstates the line's response to local widening. On the intrusion side it is a fair guide.

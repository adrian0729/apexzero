# E0: five seeds × 1B steps on `generator-v1` and `sim-v1` miss the gate (median 8 of 12 R-dev circuits, in-sample for widths)

Results of 28 Oct 2026. The run plan is [`docs/plan/e0.md`](../plan/e0.md), fixed by the tag
`e0-train-v1` (`5edb66f`) before any run. The gate (#10) was fixed earlier still. **Every R-dev
number in this note is in-sample for widths**: E0 trained on the R-dev circuits' own width
profiles (`generator-v1`, [`gw4.md`](gw4.md); [`gw3.md`](gw3.md) Section 1 lists what the label
covers). R_TEST and `outputs/sealed/` were never read.

## 1. The gate: FAIL

- **Counts.** Circuits completed (completion > 0.5 over 10 laps, final checkpoint), seeds 50–54:
  **4, 10, 8, 8, 6**. The **median is 8**, against a pass mark of 10. One seed of five reaches 10.
- **Run once.** `scripts/e0_gate.py gate` read the committed manifest (`f012062`) and wrote
  `outputs/e0/gate.json` with a lock that is never removed (`c4b8b54`).
- **No run was missing.** All five finished, with no retry, so no seed counted 0 by rule.
- Seed 51 completed Hockenheim on exactly half its laps (0.5). That is not more than 0.5, so it is
  not counted. Counting it would make seed 51's count 11 and leave the median at 8.

## 2. By circuit (descriptive)

Completion is nearly all-or-nothing: of the 60 seed × circuit results, 58 are 0 or 1.

- **The gap to the s-OCP lap** on completed circuits: a median of 6.4% over the 36 completions,
  5.1–11.7%. Each seed's median is 6.2–6.6%.
- **Against GW-3's 200M networks** (not a test: other seeds, steps and width models). GW-3's
  copied-width fold arms completed 6.8–7.7 of 12 on average and its stat-model arms 9.3–9.9
  (`gw3.md` Section 10). E0's mean, 7.2, sits with the copied-width arms, and its hardest circuits
  (Norisring, Yas Marina, Melbourne, Sochi) are the ones GW-3 found hard for copied widths. E0
  ran five times the steps, and it copies all 12 circuits' widths, not one fold's.

## 3. The run

- **The box.** One `massedcompute_A6000_plus` ($0.68/h) at 5 lanes, at `e0-train-v1`. The box
  ran 19:47:47–22:35:29 UTC, **2.80 GPU-h** (about $1.90), within the approved 2.8–3.2 (stop
  rule 3.84). Training ran 20:17:22–22:34:05 UTC, at about 100k steps/s per seed early and about
  125k later.
- **The checks.** The box's sha256 list (109 files: 80 checkpoints, 5 `eval_rdev.json`, the 12
  R-dev centrelines and 12 R-dev OCP files) matched every pulled file, and
  `round_manifest.py check` passed with nothing tolerated. The manifest ran before the box was
  deleted (`docs/plan/e0.md` Section 2).

## 4. What it means

- **The gate is not met.** `generator-v1` and `sim-v1` stay frozen (#11: "freeze
  `generator-v1` whatever the result"), and E0's five networks are the final agent's candidates
  as they are.
- **Open (#14):** whether to go ahead with the lockbox now that E0 has missed the gate, and
  whether to split R_TEST 6 + 6. Both are asked before `protocol-v1` is written. LB-1's
  checkpoint choice (#15) is unaffected.
- **Limits.** Five seeds; one checkpoint per seed (the final, as fixed: GW-1 found R-dev
  completion peaks at slow early checkpoints); 10 laps per circuit with the default car.
